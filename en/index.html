FAQ

Questions international travelers ask most

Is the suite completely private?

Yes. The suite has independent private access. You enjoy full privacy in your accommodation, with access to the estate’s outdoor pool and gardens shared only with the owners, not other paying guests.

Is the area safe for international travelers?

Perdizes is one of São Paulo’s safest, most affluent residential districts. The property is fully gated with monitored security (including Verisure) and private street presence. Still use normal big-city common sense, as you would in any major metro.

Can I pay with international credit cards or Apple Pay?

Yes. Booking via Airbnb or Booking.com accepts major international cards and the payment options those platforms support in your country (including Apple Pay where available).

How do I get there from GRU Airport?

We recommend Uber Black or a licensed airport taxi. Typical ride time is about 45-55 minutes depending on traffic. After you book, we share exact arrival instructions privately.

Am I renting the whole mansion?

No. You book a private suite inside a living family estate. That is the product: exclusivity of one suite, not a vacant whole-house rental. Outdoor leisure areas (pool, gardens, Koi pond, gym) are part of the stay experience.
